    Plates XX–XXXVIII

    Monuments

    19 elevations drawn to true scale and set in their surroundings. Visitors walk past at true size, and the white figure marks 1.7 m. Hover a part to identify it; the dashed line reads height. The Day, Sunset and Night buttons relight every scene.

    About the drawings. Spans, heights and lengths come from published figures (Wikipedia articles, ASI and project releases, checked October 2026). Profiles are simplified: truss panels, cable counts and pier spacing are schematic, and figures marked ≈ are indicative where no official value is published, such as deck clearances on several bridges.

    The map is a stylised outline for orientation only; it is not to scale and is not an authoritative boundary.

    No images are used. Every scene, silhouette and vehicle is drawn with SVG and HTML. Use the selector to view a single structure, and the full-screen button on any plate for a larger view.
